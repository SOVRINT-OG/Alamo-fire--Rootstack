Any research or scholarly reference to SOVRINT™ material must identify Katrina Pietroniro, the referenced work or repository, and its version or date; include its DOI when assigned. Citation is required for reference and does not itself grant permission to reproduce, adapt, distribute, or otherwise reuse material. Applicable statutory exceptions and separately granted written permissions remain in force.
